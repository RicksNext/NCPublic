What is Threads?

Threads is a new social media platform from Meta, the company formerly known as Facebook. Threads is a place where you can share text updates and join public conversations with people who share your interests, just like you would on Twitter. You can log in using your Instagram account and post up to 500 characters with links, photos, and videos.

But how do you know how many people are following your Threads account? How do you keep track of your growth and
success on this new platform?

The answer is NextCounts, the ultimate platform for real-time live counts for Threads and other social media platforms. NextCounts lets you see the live follower count of any Threads
account. You can also compare two or more accounts or posts side by side and see who is leading the race!


With NextCounts, you can:

·  Support your favorite Threads creators by watching their live follower count grow
·  Analyze the performance and engagement of any Threads account
·  Have fun with the interactive and simple design of NextCounts


NextCounts is the best way to enjoy Threads in real-time. Whether you are a casual fan or a serious marketer, NextCounts will help you stay on top of the Threads game. Try it out today  and see for yourself!

NextCounts was also the first ever platform to have live counters for Threads, making it a unique and innovative tool for anyone who wants to monitor their progress on this new platform. Don’t miss this opportunity to join the NextCounts community and get ahead of the curve!
