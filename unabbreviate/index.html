Get started now!

We created an easy tutorial showing how you can add your channel! Just follow along:

Step 1

Go to studio.youtube.com and click on “Settings”.

Step 2

If you're using a Brand Account, click “Move Permissions”. Otherwise, go to the next step.

Step 3

Click the “Invite” button in the permissions section.

Step 4

Enter: nextcountsbot@gmail.com and choose “Viewer (limited)” access.

Step 5

Click “Save” to confirm the invite.

Step 6

You'll be prompted to verify it's you — enter your password and 2FA if prompted. This is a one-time step.

Step 7

Our bot will accept the invite soon. Once done, you'll see our profile in the list. We only read views and sub counts — nothing else.

You're Done!

Within 24h, your full, verified Subscriber Count will be visible on NextCounts — complete with a verified badge!



FAQ

Is this allowed by YouTube?

Yes! While YouTube does not encourage creators to invite accounts they don't know into their YouTube Studio for obvious reasons (channel hijacking for example), the NextCounts Bot only makes your Studio analytics available in the website. When users invites the NextCounts Bot with more permissions than "Viewer (Limited)", the bot rejects the invite, so if you invited the bot and can't see your channel here, invite it again with the correct permissions!

Are there any risks with adding my channel in here?

No! You can be 100% certain there won't be any leaks of revenue or anything similar since the bot does not access any endpoints/analytics related to creator revenue. The bot also has 2-Factor-Authentication enabled, so no one can get access to the bot.

Do I need to install anything?

Absolutely not!  The bot invite is all that's necessary to display your full subscriber count!

Where can I see all channels unabbreviated?

You can check all channels that have joined our system in the Top Channels list!

I don't have more than 1.000 subscribers. How can I help?

There are many ways you can help the project grow and get known by more people! You can invite your favorite creators to the system and see if they like the idea of watching themselves hit milestones with their viewers live with more than what YouTube offers, in a platform trusted by thousands of creators and fans around the globe!

Just message us on Twitter and we'll reply to you as soon as possible! If you want to know about any other upcoming features or updates to the website, follow us on our Insider account where we post sneak peeks of features in the works!
